# Memorize Today's Open Heavens with Francisca

A simple, free way to hide God's word in your heart.

**Open it here:** https://franciscaoganyaoroge.github.io/memorize/

No login, no app. It works in the browser on any phone or computer.

## What you can do

- **Today's verse.** Study each day's Open Heavens memory verse. When Francisca shares the daily link on WhatsApp, tapping it adds the verse to your page.
- **Personal study.** Add any verses you want to learn yourself. Pick the book, chapter and verse, and the text fills in for the KJV, NHEB, ASV, BBE and YLT. For other versions such as the NKJV, paste the verse in yourself.
- **Practice step by step:**
  1. **Read.** Read the verse out loud a few times, saying the reference before and after.
  2. **Fade.** Words disappear a little at a time while you recite it.
  3. **Letters.** Only the first letter of each word is left.
  4. **Test.** Type the first letter of each word from memory.
  5. **Where?** Name the book, chapter and verse.
- **"Where is it found?" quiz.** A mixed quiz across all your verses to learn their references.
- **Games.** Verse Builder, Missing Word, Match-Up, Beat the Clock, Books of the Bible and Where Is It Found, with a daily practice streak.
- **Weekly review.** A test on every verse from the past 7 days, with a weekly streak.
- **Study notes.** Write what each verse means to you.
- **WhatsApp status pictures.** Turn any verse into 4 status pictures (read, fill in the blanks, first letters, say it from memory) so friends can learn it just by tapping through your status.

Your name, verses, scores and notes are saved on your own phone only. Nobody else can see them. Use **Backup & restore** to move them to a new phone.

## Bible text

The built-in Bible versions (KJV, NHEB, ASV, BBE, YLT) are in the public domain. Copyrighted versions such as the NKJV are not included; you can paste verses from them for your own study.
