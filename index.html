Backup & restore

Your verses, notes, scores and streak are saved on this phone only. Make a backup before you change phones.

On iPhone, choose Save to Files (iCloud Drive), or send it to yourself on WhatsApp. On your new phone, open this page and tap Restore from backup.

How it works

  1. Read: read the verse out loud 3–5 times.
  2. Fade: words disappear a little at a time while you recite it.
  3. First letters: only the first letter of each word is left.
  4. Test: type the first letter of each word from memory.
  5. Where?: name the book, chapter and verse.

WhatsApp status pictures

For each picture, tap Save, then choose Save Image to put it in your camera roll.

  1. Press and hold each picture and choose Save to Photos. If that doesn't appear, tap the picture to open it full screen and take a screenshot.
  2. In WhatsApp, go to Updates → My status and add them in order, 1 to 4.
